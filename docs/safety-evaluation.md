# VeriPool 2.o — safety evaluation

Generated by `npm run eval:safety` (seed 20261022, 400 simulated trips per row, GPS sample every 3 s).
Trips run between random pairs of the 100 real hotspots (2–25 km apart) along a simulated winding road.
GPS noise: σ = 8 m, with stretches of σ = 25 m multipath and 2% sudden 80 m jumps; normal trips also stop at traffic about every 2 km (at least 300 m apart) for 20–150 s.

| Scenario | Profile | Detected | Median delay | 90th pct delay |

| wrong way | day | 95.5% | 105 s | 108 s |
| wrong way | night | 99.8% | 51 s | 54 s |
| long stop | day | 75.0% | 348 s | 750 s |
| long stop | night | 93.0% | 231 s | 615 s |

Known limitation: stop-and-go jams where the car stands still for more than 3 min at night (5 min by day) will trigger a check-in. That is deliberate — answering costs the rider one tap.

Long stops are not flagged within 150 m of a hotspot or within 1 km of the drop-off, so some trips that stop near one are correctly left alone.

## 5. Fare guarantee — property test

20,000 random rides (1–6 legs, 0–4 existing bookings of 1–3 seats, then one more booking):

- existing riders whose fare went **up** after a new booking: **0**
- rides where the driver's own share was zero or negative (i.e. the driver profited): **0**

Why it holds: on every leg the cost is split as cost ÷ (seats on board + 1). A new booking only adds seats to the legs it covers, so each share on those legs can only shrink, and the "+1" is the driver, who always carries a share.
